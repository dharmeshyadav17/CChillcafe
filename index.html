About Beechwoods Cafe

A Warm Rest Stop in the Heart of the Tasman District.

Nestled quietly on Waller Street in Murchison, Beechwoods Cafe is the classic New Zealand stopover where rustic charm meets heartwarming flavors. Whether you are driving between Nelson, Christchurch, and the wild West Coast or exploring the Kahurangi National Park, we are here to revive your travel spirits.

From freshly pulled espresso using premium beans to flaky homemade pies, freshly buttered scones, gourmet sandwiches, and wholesome all-day breakfast plates, every dish is prepared with genuine country warmth.

"Kia Ora! Welcome to our little corner of Murchison."

When you step into Beechwoods Cafe, our promise is simple: a warm greeting, freshly baked treats ready in the cabinet, piping hot coffee, and a cozy space to rest your feet before continuing your adventure. Thank you to every traveler and local who has supported us over the years!"
