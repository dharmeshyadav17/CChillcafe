A place to slow down

Chim Chill Coffee & Food is a neighborhood café nestled in the hills of Suthep. We believe in quality over speed — from our carefully sourced single origin beans to the pastries baked fresh each morning.

We roast our own coffee on-site twice a week and partner with local farms for our ingredients. Our space was designed to feel like an extension of your own living room: warm wooden tables, plenty of natural light, and soft background jazz.
